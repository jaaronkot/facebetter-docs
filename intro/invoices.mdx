---
title: "Invoices and tax details"
description: "Add your company name, billing address and tax ID so invoices are ready for tax filing."
---

Payments are processed by Stripe, and every successful payment issues an invoice. The invoice shows the billing details saved on your customer record.

## What appears on an invoice

- **Name**: the full name or legal business name you enter
- **Billing address**: the address you enter at checkout or in the billing portal
- **Tax ID**: the business tax ID you add, if any

You can view and download all invoices on the Console **Billing** page.

## Enter details at checkout

1. On the Stripe checkout page, open **Billing details** and enter your company's legal name and full billing address. Use the country where your company is registered.
2. If that country supports business tax IDs, select **I'm purchasing as a business**, then enter your legal business name and tax ID.
3. Finish the payment. These details are saved and shown on this and all later invoices.

The same fields are collected for one-time payments such as Alipay, WeChat Pay and lifetime licenses. The address is always collected; the tax ID form appears only for supported countries.

Tax ID collection at checkout is available in many countries, including Turkey, EU member states, the United Kingdom, the UAE, Australia and Canada. The form appears automatically based on the billing country you select.

<Note>
The tax ID field is for business tax IDs only. For Turkey, enter your TR TIN; for EU countries, your VAT number, and so on.
</Note>

## Add or update details later

If the tax ID form isn't available for your billing country at checkout (for example, China), you can add it after paying:

1. Open the Console **Billing** page.
2. Click **Manage subscription**, then choose the billing details option to open the Stripe billing portal.
3. Under **Billing information**, update the name, billing address or tax ID. To add a tax ID, choose the ID type, enter the number and save. Use **Add another ID** if you need more than one.

<Warning>
Changes apply only to invoices issued after you save. Invoices that have already been issued are final and cannot be edited. Enter or check your details before the first payment.
</Warning>

If you need an already-issued invoice corrected, contact support.
